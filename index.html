Things to do

For low-key wedding weekend downtime, Old Town Temecula is good for walking around, Temecula Wine Country has plenty of wineries nearby, and there are several easy activity options around the area.

Old Town Temecula

Walkable historic district with shops, restaurants, patios, tasting rooms, and western-style storefronts. Good low-effort outdoor stop for strolling before dinner or between wedding weekend plans.

Check Out the Wineries

Temecula Wine Country has many tasting rooms, vineyard patios, and scenic spots nearby. Check hours and reservations if you want to visit during the wedding weekend.

Activities

A few options for guests who want something outdoorsy, relaxing, or more resort-style during the wedding weekend.

  • Temecula spa options
